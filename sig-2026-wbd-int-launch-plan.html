Flags

  • Email address (decided Oct 24): concierge@ for customers who ordered and have questions or concerns about delivered products (all order emails); contact@ for everything else.
  • Payments (decided Oct 24): Shop Pay and PayPal at launch. No Klarna. Afterpay after launch. The Privacy Policy and the Figma PDP payment line still mention Klarna/Afterpay; fix with Tuesday's copy.
  • Damage window: the Delivered email copy says 30 days to report damage; the policy says 48 hours. The order email kit uses 48 hours.
  • Fonts in email: Royals and PP Playground only show if their licenses cover email and they are added to Klaviyo; otherwise Georgia.
  • Transactional approval: not needed while Shopify sends the order emails. Only ask Klaviyo support if a Klaviyo flow must reach people who haven't subscribed.
  • Brand email defaults in Klaviyo are out of date (Kepler, Helvetica, blue links). New emails should start from SIG 2026 / Base, not a blank template.
  • Old footer: the RSVP emails' "Footer 001" block has no mailing address. The new SIG 2026 / Footer / Text adds it, plus Manage preferences.
  • No copy yet for browse abandonment, refill reminder, win-back or the sign-up popup.
  • Customer account and password reset emails are Shopify's, not Klaviyo's (copy tab notes).
