Are customs duties collected at checkout, or billed to the customer on delivery?

8
Chargeback risk

Does no refunds apply to damaged goods too, or only to undamaged returns?

Store credit or exchange only is clear for a customer who simply changed their mind. If a marble piece arrives broken and the customer is offered store credit instead of money back, that is the single most likely source of a card chargeback.

Recommendation. Carve damaged and defective goods out of the no refunds rule. Refunding a rare broken piece costs far less than losing the dispute and the processing fee.
9
Affects backend setup

How does store credit actually work?

Shopify store credit or a gift card code? Does it expire? Does it cover the shipping cost on the exchange, or does the customer pay that again?

Cancellations

What is the actual cancellation cutoff?

The notes say changes and cancellations are not guaranteed once fulfillment has begun. A customer cannot read that and know where they stand. We need something concrete, for example within two hours of ordering, or any time before a shipping label is created.

Which products, if any, are final sale?

Nothing has been designated final sale. The Signature Edit dermaplane and the Concierge toothbrush are hygiene-contact products. Confirm whether an opened unit is returnable at all, and whether the blades and refills are returnable once the seal is broken.

Why it matters. There is a final sale flag built into the product template already. It stays off until you confirm which pieces carry it.

One recommendation, not a question

The 48-hour marble damage window is a decision on record, with the rationale of separating freight damage from customer-caused damage. That rationale holds at seven days just as well as at two. Forty-eight hours punishes a customer who was traveling when the box landed, and it is the clause most likely to read as unreasonable on an international order.

The change costs almost nothing operationally and makes the policy easier to defend. Worth an attorney's read either way, since it is the one term with real money behind it.
